Material consistency

Website backgrounds describe the full-page atmosphere. Board themes are tuned separately for hex readability, so similarly named materials may use different brightness and contrast on the playable board.

Recommended default pairing

Brushed Gunmetal + Honed Slate

Gunmetal keeps the website shell serious and low-noise. Honed Slate gives the 85-hex board a tactile surface with enough separation for black and white pieces while cyan legal moves and red attacks remain immediate.
