01 · Goal

Break the opponent’s Keep.

Capture the opponent’s Keep before your own Keep falls. The Keep is the highest-value objective on the board.

02 · Turn

Take up to two moves, then confirm.

Fortress separates planning from commitment. You can make one or two moves, review the state, undo if needed, then confirm the turn.

07 · Match resolution

Know how a game can end.

Fortress starts with 16 pieces per side on an 85-hex board. Match resolution stays explicit so players always know why play has ended.

End states
♛Keep capturedDestroy the opponent’s Keep to win.
×Mutual destructionIf both Keeps fall in the same combat, the game is a draw.
↘ResignationA player may concede the match.
◇StalemateThe side to move has no legal move.
✓AgreementBoth players accept a draw offer.
◷TimeoutA player’s clock runs out.
What to noticeThe interface should always explain the result, not just stop the game.
